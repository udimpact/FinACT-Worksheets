Asset Allocation: The “Rule of 100”

A simple classroom shortcut for understanding why time horizon can matter when thinking about risk.

Why does age appear in this rule?

A younger person may have more time before long-term goals and therefore more time to recover from market ups and downs. As age increases, the rule gradually shifts more toward stability. It is only a starting idea, not a complete investment plan.
100−Age=?% growth side
Rule-of-100 growth side—Higher-growth / higher-volatility side
Rule-of-100 stable side—Lower-volatility / stability side

Learning note: this shortcut does not know your goals, debt, emergency fund, responsibilities, income stability, knowledge or personal risk tolerance. Those factors matter in real financial decisions.
